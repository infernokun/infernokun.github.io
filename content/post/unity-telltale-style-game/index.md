A work-in-progress point and click game in the style of Telltale's adventures, like The Walking Dead and Tales from the Borderlands.

It's purely for learning, with no plans for any kind of release. Building something in Telltale's style is a way to learn how their games are put together: conversation choices, characters you walk around with, and scenes that react to what you picked.

More to come one day.
